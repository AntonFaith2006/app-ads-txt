The reason behind it

Make a living.
Don’t forget to live.

ReWyld Outdoors is not a polished “perfect life outside” feed. Shaun’s content is strongest when it feels like you are there for the trip—the good bits, the gear that failed, the weather, the lesson and the story that came out of it.

Entertain. Give people a reason to watch.
Inspire. Make them want to get outside themselves.
Instruct. Leave them knowing something useful.
